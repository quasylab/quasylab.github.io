QuasyLab studies formal methods for specifying, analysing and building concurrent and distributed systems. These systems are often large-scale and sometimes cyber-physical. Our work has four strands.

### Languages for system behaviour

We design (domain-specific) languages for specifying the behaviour of concurrent and distributed, possibly cyber-physical, large-scale systems.

### Logics for system properties

We study formal languages, such as modal and temporal logics, for specifying the qualitative and quantitative properties of these systems.

### Tools for analysis

We develop tools that support the analysis of systems specified with our languages and logics.

### Applications

We apply our frameworks to support the development of real-life applications.
